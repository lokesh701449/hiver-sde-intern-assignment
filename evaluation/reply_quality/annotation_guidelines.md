# Reply-Quality Human Annotation Guidelines

## Overview
Evaluate the generated AmazonHelp customer support response (or escalation action) on a 1-5 scale for 5 core dimensions plus overall quality.

## Rating Scale
- **1 = Poor**: Completely incorrect, unhelpful, misleading, or unsafe.
- **2 = Weak**: Major omissions, vague, or sub-optimal tone/action.
- **3 = Acceptable**: Bare minimum acceptable support response or appropriate escalation routing.
- **4 = Good**: Relevant, helpful, polite, and well-grounded response.
- **5 = Excellent**: Flawless support response, empathetic, concise, and perfectly actionable.

## Rating Dimensions
1. **Relevance**: Does the response address the customer's actual issue?
2. **Groundedness**: Is the response consistent with historical AmazonHelp evidence without making fake promises?
3. **Helpfulness**: Does it provide clear next steps or appropriate escalation routing?
4. **Tone**: Is it polite, professional, and empathetic?
5. **Safety / Escalation**: Does it avoid falsely claiming private account lookups were done publicly? If escalation is needed, does it direct the customer properly?
6. **Overall Quality**: Is this a reasonable first-draft response for a human agent?
